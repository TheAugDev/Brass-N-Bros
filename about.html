Our Story, Our Discipline

Brass N Bros is committed to training the responsible firearm owners of tomorrow using real-world standards and clear instruction.

Authority Rooted in Professional Experience

The Brass N Bros Academy is built on the foundation of **practical, high-stakes firearms handling.** Our instruction methodology is informed by experience gained in the **U.S. Navy (including ASF duties)** and current training in **Law Enforcement**. We teach the same discipline, safety standards, and real-world proficiency that professionals rely on. This is training designed not just for the square range, but for confidence in any scenario.

We skip the competitive fluff and focus strictly on repeatable, efficient, and **safe operational handling**. If you are looking for instruction that is clear, high-quality, and rooted in proven professional standards, you are in the right place.
